## Relevant patterns to compare against the current app

The current app should prioritize fast touch-friendly checkout, clear payment choices, inventory visibility, low-stock alerts, customer and supplier ledgers, transaction reports, receipt and invoice sharing, barcode/QR lookup, returns, stock adjustments, backup/restore, and role-based access.

For dairy operations, useful patterns include morning/evening collection, FAT/SNF-based calculation, configurable billing cycles, advances deducted from payouts, offline collection, and farmer payment reminders.

For this project, only patterns relevant to the existing Gajurmukhi requirements should be implemented. Marketing claims, unrelated industry features, and unsupported payment gateways should not be added without a concrete backend or device integration.
